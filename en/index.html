Frequently asked questions

How do I book?

By phone, 7 days a week: Sandy on +33 6 77 79 72 72 or Sandra on +33 6 46 58 48 47.

Where is the treatment room?

Inside the Sunrise Beach Hôtel, 1 avenue des Courlis, Gruissan, facing Plage des Chalets. Get directions.

What happens during a session?

Each massage is adapted to your needs, your pain and how you feel. You talk it through with Sandy or Sandra before you begin.

What if I need to cancel?

Please let us know at least 24 hours in advance, by phone.

Can I have a massage while pregnant?

Yes, that is what the Sérénita pregnancy and postnatal treatment (60 min, €80) is for. Talk to your midwife or doctor before your appointment.

Are the massages suitable for children?

Yes. Sérénita massages young and old alike, from babies to adults, including people with disabilities.

Can I give a massage as a gift?

Yes, with a gift voucher valid for 1 year, paid at the salon by cash or card.

How much does a massage cost?

From €30 for a 20-minute treatment to €140 for the Sérénita Duo. All prices are on the treatment menu.

Are these medical treatments?

No. They are well-being treatments: they do not replace medical advice or treatment.
